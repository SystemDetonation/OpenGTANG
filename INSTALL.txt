OpenGTANG 2.85 - install
========================
OpenGTANG is a fork of GTA x Beam by Treto420 (https://github.com/Treto420/GTANG, MIT licence).
Drive BeamNG.drive's cars and bikes in GTA V's world. Story mode only.
Full guide: README.md, and the original mod's guide in ORIGINAL_README.md.
Home: https://github.com/SystemDetonation/OpenGTANG

YOU NEED
- GTA V Legacy for PC (Steam, Rockstar Games Launcher or Epic). Not the Enhanced edition.
- Script Hook V, from http://www.dev-c.com/gtav/scripthookv/ (not included).
- BeamNG.drive 0.40 or newer.

1. SCRIPT HOOK V (once)
   From Script Hook V's "bin" folder, copy ScriptHookV.dll and dinput8.dll into your GTA V folder
   (the folder with GTA5.exe). Usually:
     Steam:    C:\Program Files (x86)\Steam\steamapps\common\Grand Theft Auto V
               (or in Steam: right-click GTA V > Manage > Browse local files)
     Rockstar: C:\Program Files\Rockstar Games\Grand Theft Auto V
     Epic:     C:\Program Files\Epic Games\GTAV

2. GTA V PART
   Copy everything in this zip's GTA folder into your GTA V folder (next to GTA5.exe):
     GtaBeamD1.asi        the OpenGTANG loader (Script Hook V loads it)
     GtaBeamD1_core.dll   the mod itself (both files are needed)
     GtaBeamD1.ini        settings
     GtaBeamD1_*.json     game content (props, ground grip, traffic, weapons)

3. BEAMNG.DRIVE PART
   Paste this into Explorer's address bar and press Enter (make the "unpacked" folder if it's missing):
     %LOCALAPPDATA%\BeamNG\BeamNG.drive\current\mods\unpacked\
   Copy this zip's BeamNG\gtabridge folder into it, so you get ...\mods\unpacked\gtabridge\lua\...
   In BeamNG's Mods menu, check that gtabridge is enabled.

4. GTA SETTING
   Turn MSAA OFF in GTA's graphics settings (with it on, the car looks dark or see-through).

FIRST DRIVE
   1. Start BeamNG first. Load the level "Grid, Small, Pure" and spawn your car or bike.
   2. Start GTA V in story mode. A message says the mod has loaded.
   3. Press F7 and pick "Get car from BeamNG" (the first export takes a while).
   4. Press F10 to drive with your own BeamNG controls. F10 again stops.

UPDATING (from an older OpenGTANG, or from GTA x Beam 2.77 / 2.83)
   Back up your GtaBeamD1.ini if you changed settings. Copy the GTA files over the old ones (say yes to
   replacing; GtaBeamD1_core.dll is new). Copy gtabridge over the old one in mods\unpacked and restart
   BeamNG. Put your own values back in the new ini (an old ini also still works).

KEYS
   F7 menu | Ctrl+Shift+F7 restart the mod without closing GTA | Shift+F11 get car | F11 put on / off
   F10 drive | Ctrl+Shift+F10 seat adjust | Ctrl+F9 crash damage to you | Ctrl+F11 reload the ini

IF IT DOESN'T WORK
   GTA V folder: ScriptHookV.log (Script Hook V), GtaBeamD1_loader.log (the loader), GtaBeamD1.log (the mod).
   BeamNG: %LOCALAPPDATA%\BeamNG\BeamNG.drive\current\gtabridge\gtabridge_log.txt
   Bug reports with both GtaBeamD1.log and gtabridge_log.txt: https://github.com/SystemDetonation/OpenGTANG/issues
